# Date: 09/09/2025

## Purpose:

To review design improvements for the cloud chamber cooling system, focusing on hot-side cooling (air vs water closed-loop), chamber construction materials, temperature measurement devices, and copper plate thickness.

## Literature review

* Hot-side cooling:
    * Conducted literature review on closed-loop water cooling for Peltier hot sides.
    * References:
        * Uncovering the Cooling Potential by Water Circulation on the Hot Side of a Peltier Module → [link](https://www.mdpi.com/2673-4591/45/1/34)
        * Enhanced Peltier refrigerator using an innovative hot-side heat rejection mechanism → [link](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0360544224029074) (kind of confusing)
    * Takeaway: Water cooling shows strong theoretical benefits, but the current air cooling setup is not yet a bottleneck. Keep air cooling for now, but water cooling may be a future upgrade if hot-side heat extraction becomes limiting.
* Chamber materials:
    * Compared acrylic vs. glass using references:
        * CERN S’Cool LAB DIY Cloud Chamber Manual → [link](https://scoollab.web.cern.ch/sites/default/files/documents/20200521_JW_DIYManual_CloudChamber_v7.pdf)
        * Building-Cloud-Chamber (Practical notes from University of Liverpool) → [link](https://www.liverpool.ac.uk/~sdb/CloudChamber/Building-Cloud-Chamber.pdf)
        * PhysicsOpenLab Cloud Chamber Manual → [link](https://physicsopenlab.org/2017/05/05/cloud-chamber/)
    * Takeaway: None of the references mentioned any significant differences between using acrylic or glass for the chamber. Proceed with glass chamber due to availability, durability, and fast delivery. 
* Temperature measurement:
    * Identified inconsistencies with the FLIR thermal camera.
    * Selected 10 kΩ NTC thermistors ([Amazon](https://www.amazon.com.au/dp/B0CJ7T56VS?ref=ppx_yo2ov_dt_b_fed_asin_title)) as replacement for continuous, reliable logging.
    * Considering DIY solutions such as the 10-channel thermistor voltage logger (Curious Scientist) → [link](https://curiousscientist.tech/blog/10-channel-thermistor-voltage-logger).
    * Takeaway: Reliance on the FLIR camera is problematic; thermistors (and potentially a multichannel logger) will provide more consistent and continuous data.
* Copper plate thickness:
    * Searched for literature on the effect of copper plate thickness on cooling performance but did not find relevant studies.
    * Takeaway: Lack of existing studies on copper plate thickness gives us opportunity to do direct experimental testing. Will be switching from the current 2 mm plate to 1 mm and comparing cooling rate and minimum temperatures.

## Decisions:

* Will find a ready made Glass chamber
* Will order NTC thermistors 
* Might explore thermistor logger option → ordering the simple NTC thermistor for now
* Will find 1 mm copper plate options to order 
* Sticking to air cooling for now
