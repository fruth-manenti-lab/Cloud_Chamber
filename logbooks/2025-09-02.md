**Date: 02/09/2025**

## Overview

Continued testing of the cloud chamber cooling system with focus on temperature measurement accuracy and heat dissipation.

## What worked?

* Hot side of Peltier cells monitored:
  * Peltier Cell 1 exposed edges reached 73.8 °C
  <img src="logbooks/pictures/2025-09-02/thermal_image_1.png" alt="Thermal image" width="600">
  * Peltier Cell 2 exposed edges reached 53.3 °C
  <img src="logbooks/pictures/2025-09-02/thermal_image_2.png" alt="Thermal image" width="600">
* Copper plate temperature plateaued at –13.3 °C, consistent with previous runs.

## Issues Identified

* Hot side of Peltier cells still too hot → poor thermal contact with heat sinks caused probably due to inconsistent thermal paste application.
* Cold side underperforming since maximum temperature differential achievable by TEC2-19006 is 80 °C → copper plate not reaching target temperatures.
* FLIR thermal camera limitations:
  * Observed inconsistent readings when switching the camera off/on (temperature temporarily “dropped” to –14.4 °C, then returned to –13.3 °C).
  * Camera range –20 °C to 150 °C, making accurate measurements below –30 °C impossible.

## Next steps:

* Reapply thermal paste carefully between Peltier cells and heat sinks to improve heat dissipation.
* Acquire thermistors for accurate, continuous logging of copper plate temperature, especially below –20 °C.
* Re-test system to see if improved thermal contact help reach copper plate temperature goals
