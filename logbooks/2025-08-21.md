# Issues Identified

* The Peltier cells (V_max = 16V & I_max = 6A per cell) are connected in series → can’t be connected in parallel because the power supply is limited to 5A. 
* The fans (V_max = 12V & I_max = 0.4A per fan) are connected in parallel → works well but if we connect it to the Peltier cells (that are running on 30V) in parallel then it would be fried.
