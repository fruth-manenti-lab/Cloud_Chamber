## Overview

Cooling System Reassembly and Thermal Paste Optimization

## Purpose

To test whether reapplying thermal paste and reassembling the cooling stack reduces thermal resistance and improves cold-side performance. Previous runs plateaued around –14.4 °C after 20 minutes; target is –26 to –30 °C.

## Method

* Disassembled the cloud chamber cooling system.
* Reapplied thermal paste to all interfaces (Peltier → copper plate, Peltier → heat sinks).
* Reassembled with top plate, copper plate, and dual heat sinks.
* Powered system using bench supply.
* Recorded copper plate temperature at intervals using FLIR thermal camera.
* Monitored hot-side temperatures with IR probe.

## Results

**Observations**

* Initial power-on: only one Peltier drawing current → traced to alligator clip connection. After correction, both cells drew expected current.
* Cooling response was significantly faster compared to previous runs.

### Comparison to previous best run:

* Reached –19.9 °C at 25 min (improvement of ~5.5 °C over previous –14.4 °C at 20 min).
* Faster early-stage cooling (–7.6 °C at 8 min vs –5.0 °C previously).

## Issues Identified

* Cold side still plateauing ~ –20 °C → not yet reaching target range (–26 to –30 °C). Plateau may be due to:
    * Inadequate heat extraction on hot side. → ruled out (heatsinks remain ~30 °C, hot side stable at ~46 °C, within expected limits).
    * Thermal paste not perfectly spread under load.
* Power delivery not optimal (series configuration limits performance).
* FLIR camera: temporary inconsistencies still present when switching on/off.
* FLIR export failed with “server not connected” error; unable to save images.
