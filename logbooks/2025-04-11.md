# Overview

Testing the performance of two TEC2-19006 Peltier cells connected in series, intended for the cloud chamber cooling system.

# Test Setup

* Power source: 24V, 6A initially
* Tools used: Thermal camera (borrowed from Kevin, first-year lab)
* Configuration: Two Peltier cells in series, copper plate mounted above

# Initial Observations

* Working Peltier cell: ~–18°C
* Suspected faulty cell: ~–12°C

Tengriz noted that the voltage calculation used was incorrect — it was meant for another setup.

# Voltage Correction

* Switched to a variable power supply
* Voltage increased to 31.0V (since each cell can take up to 15.7V), current draw: ~3.64A

# New temperatures:

* Working cell: < –40°C
* Faulty cell: 3/4 of the “Faulty” Peltier cell reached –24°C ; the upper right side remained at 23°C (room temperature).

# Copper Plate Temperature Readings

* Above working cell: –13°C
* Above faulty cell: –2°C
* Centre of the plate: ~3°C

# Issues Identified

* Faulty Peltier cell clearly underperforming
* Thermal paste was insufficient, causing ice formation between cell and copper plate. This likely caused poor thermal conduction.
* Measurements from 09/04 likely unreliable due to this.

# Expectation

With the faulty cell replaced and proper insulation:

* Expected plate temp: –26°C to –30°C
* Confident in meeting cooling requirements for the cloud chamber
